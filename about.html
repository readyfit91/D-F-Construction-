Our Story

Built on Integrity,
Driven by Craft

D&F Construction is a family-owned and operated business built on the belief that every project deserves the same care and attention we'd give our own home. With over a decade of hands-on experience in construction and remodeling, we've earned the trust of homeowners and businesses across the St. Louis area.

From our first project to today, our approach has never changed: do honest work, communicate openly, and never cut corners. We treat every job site like it's our own property and every client like they're family.

Whether it's a kitchen renovation, new construction, or a commercial buildout, we bring the same level of dedication and craftsmanship to every project we take on.
